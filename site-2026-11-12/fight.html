HOW TO FIGHT

Dodge with ◀ ▶ (or swipe left and right). Block by holding ▼ (or swipe down).
B throws your left and A your right. Hold ▲ to aim at the head; otherwise you go to the body. On the touch screen, tap high or low on your opponent.

Watch for the glint: a sparkle on the glove means a punch is coming. Uppercuts and haymakers can't be blocked, so dodge them. Dodge or counter at the right moment to earn ⭐. Press ⚡ POWER to spend your stars on a power punch.

Hearts are your stamina. You lose them when your punches get blocked or you get hit, and you can't punch until you get your wind back. Three knockdowns in a round is a TKO. If nobody goes down, the judges score it 10-9 round by round.

In the Cage, ▲ + A is a head kick and ▼ + B is a leg kick.

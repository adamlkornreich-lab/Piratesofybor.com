Open World

Walk Riviera Ave and find Laura & Dom's Crossword Cabin on Cross St, cross the bridge to Jen's Island, visit Ruben's Cliff and Rene's Guitar Shack, and head down to the Dragon Table at Acropolis on the 7th Ave block. Cross the east bridge to Danny Alonso Island for the Pirate Pool & Bingo Hall, Pippy's Surf & Skate Shack, the skate park, the resort villas and the travel dock. Take the camp road west off Riviera Ave to Camp Akiba, where your best friends are waiting at the campfire. Enter the open world.

Online matchmaking

Player-vs-player across the internet arrives with crew accounts and the app-store release. Until then, the tavern games, the Mixology Duel, Pirate Pool, arm wrestling, chess, trivia and Shere's Revenge support two players on one device. Andy Golf Club, Treasure Hunt, the crossword, Jen Runs the Ship, The Schiller Ledger, The Dragon Table, Rene's Guitar School, both of Ashlee Rae's games, Skate the Grotto and Pirate Bingo (you vs the crew) are solo.

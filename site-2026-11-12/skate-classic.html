Skate the Grotto Classic

Two pirate parks: Gaspar's Docks at sunset and a night run down 7th Ave past Gaspar's Grotto. Ninety seconds per session. Ollie the cannon ramps, flip and spin in the air, slide the rope rails and chain it all into one big combo. Land clean to bank it, or bail and lose it all.

Drop in

Ollie to jump. In the air, hit Flip, Spin or hold Grab. Press Grind over a rail. Different tricks in one combo raise the multiplier.

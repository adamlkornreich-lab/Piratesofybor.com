Privacy Policy

Gaspar's Grotto Arcade, including Andy Golf Club · Effective October 30, 2026

We built these games to be played, not to collect information about you. We do not ask for an account, show ads, sell data, or track you across apps or websites.

What stays on your device

  • Your best scores, unfinished rounds and sound settings are saved in your device's own storage so they are there next time.
  • This information never leaves your device and we cannot see it. Deleting the app or clearing its data removes it.

What we do not collect

  • No names, email addresses, contacts, photos, location, or payment details.
  • No analytics, advertising identifiers, or tracking.
  • No in-app purchases in this version.

Services the app uses

  • Typefaces: when you are online, the games load fonts from Google Fonts. Google receives standard technical information such as your IP address to deliver them; see Google's privacy policy. Offline, the games use your device's built-in fonts.
  • Announcer voice: commentary uses your device's built-in text-to-speech. Nothing is recorded, and the app never uses your microphone or camera.

Children

The arcade is not directed to children under 13. The Pour-Off is a bartending game with drink names and pouring, and some trivia mentions bars and drinks.

People shown in the games

Andy, Adam, Steph, Landon and Stephania appear with their permission.

Changes and contact
